The problem

Diners decide with their eyes. Menus only give them words.

  • Unfamiliar dishes are hard to picture from a name and a line of text.

  • Portion and plate size can be unclear until the dish arrives.

  • Staff may repeat the same explanations during a busy service.

66%

of surveyed US adults preferred menus that include photos of the dishes.

Toast blind survey of 850 US adults, fielded 16 September 2024 (source). It is about food photos, not AR, and it is a US sample, so it does not describe Adelaide diners or prove demand for this product.
